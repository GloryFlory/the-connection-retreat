What This Is

Not couples therapy.
Not a tantra weekend.
Something more immediate.

The Intimacy Retreat is a structured residential experience for couples who want to invest in their relationship the way they invest in the rest of their lives — deliberately, wholeheartedly, and with skilled support.

Over two nights and two full days, Flo and Maria guide a small group of couples through practices drawn from somatic therapy, authentic relating, embodied communication, and conscious sexuality. The aim: to close the gap between who you are in private and how you actually show up with each other.

The retreat is fully clothed, consent-based, and held in a safe container. Nothing is required. Everything is invited.

The Programme

A weekend that
deepens as it moves

The retreat follows a deliberate arc — from arrival and safety-building, through honest conversation and embodied practice, toward integration and a renewed sense of what is possible between you.

01

Arrival & Ground

Friday evening · Safety · Honesty · Arrival

We begin slowly. The first evening is about landing — in the space, with the group, and with each other. We establish shared agreements and a foundation of safety. We do the most underestimated thing in any relationship: we listen. Properly. Without fixing, advising, or redirecting.

By the end of Friday evening, couples often find they have already said something to each other they've been carrying for months.

02

Depth & Desire

Saturday · Body · Voice · Longing · Trust

The full day moves through three distinct phases: somatic morning practices to drop from the mind into the body; structured desire mapping conversations in the afternoon — a guided framework for expressing what you want and truly hearing what your partner wants; and an embodied evening session focused on presence, touch, and mutual attunement.

This is the heart of the weekend. Couples describe it as both more confronting and more tender than they expected.

03

Integration & Return

Sunday morning · Ritual · Commitment · Continuation

Sunday morning is unhurried. We close the container with a ceremony that honours what has been opened. Couples name — to each other and to the group — one thing they are taking home and one practice they are committing to. We send you back to your ordinary life changed by having said things clearly.

Post-retreat integration support is included: a follow-up call with Flo or Maria in the weeks after you return.

Questions

Answered honestly,
without spin

Is anything explicit or sexual in nature?

No. The retreat is fully clothed throughout. The work touches on themes of desire, intimacy, and embodied connection — but all practices are structured, consent-based, and take place within each couple. Nothing explicit happens in the group setting. The container is safe, warm, and professionally held.

Will we have to share personal things with the whole group?

You are never required to share more than you choose to. The group container is held with strong agreements around confidentiality and respect. Many exercises are done privately within each couple, and sharing with the group is always an invitation — never a demand. What you bring to the retreat stays with the retreat.

What if we're going through a difficult patch right now?

The retreat is not designed for couples in acute crisis or requiring clinical support — in those cases we would encourage working with a couples therapist first. However, many couples come precisely because something has become stuck or distant, and the retreat creates the conditions for things to shift. If you're unsure whether it's the right fit, reach out for an honest conversation before committing.

Do we interact with the other couples in exercises?

Some exercises are done within your couple only. Others involve the wider group — authentic relating practices, shared conversations, group movement. You will be in meaningful contact with the other couples present, but the deepest work always remains between the two of you. The group provides a field of safety, not an intrusion into your relationship.

We've never done anything like this — is that okay?

It is more than okay — those who come with no prior experience often find it most transformative. You don't need a background in tantra, somatic practice, or authentic relating. The retreat is designed to meet you where you are. What matters is that both of you are genuinely willing to show up.

What kind of couples attend?

All kinds. Long-term partnerships, newer couples building intentional foundations, couples who have been through difficulty and want to reconnect, couples who are doing well and want to go deeper. There is no right profile — only a shared willingness to invest in what you have together.
